Hi, I'm Alexia. I'm a PhD candidate in chemistry at UT Austin and a DOE CSGF fellow.

I work on simulating how energy moves through molecular materials after they absorb light. The methods are formally exact, and their cost doesn't grow with system size, so we can go after big systems. My runs go on Frontier, Perlmutter and Lonestar6.

Most of my code lives in [MesoHOPS](https://github.com/MesoscienceLab/mesohops). My advisor and I are its two code owners. We review everything the lab contributes, I run the public releases, and we keep test coverage at 95%.

This past summer I was at Berkeley Lab in the Neaton group, studying how excitons and lattice vibrations interact in crystalline pentacene.
