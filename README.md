### Research

I build quantum dynamics methods, and the simulations that use them, to work out
how molecular materials move energy around after they absorb light.

- **Methods.** Nonadiabatic quantum dynamics in a formally exact framework whose
  cost does not grow with system size. As part of a subteam, we showed it stays
  flat out to a million states.
- **Simulations.** Pipelines that connect these methods to nonlinear
  spectroscopy and fluorescence microscopy of real materials.
- **National lab practicum.** Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, 2026, in
  J. B. Neaton's group: exciton and phonon dynamics in crystalline pentacene.
