PhD candidate in chemistry at UT Austin and DOE Computational Science Graduate Fellow.

I develop formally exact scalable quantum dynamics methods for energy transfer in molecular materials, and the simulations that connect them to spectroscopy and microscopy. I'm one of two code owners of [MesoHOPS](https://github.com/MesoscienceLab/mesohops), our lab's open-source library, where I review contributions and manage releases.

In summer 2026 I did a practicum at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory on exciton–phonon dynamics in crystalline pentacene with Jeffrey Neaton.

Recent paper: [Formally exact fluorescence spectroscopy simulations for mesoscale molecular aggregates with N<sup>0</sup> scaling](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0268435), J. Chem. Phys. (2025)
